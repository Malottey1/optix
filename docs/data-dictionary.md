# Synthetic data dictionary

Generated by `python -m data_gen.generate --seed 2026` (deterministic, about 2 s). Five fictional teams,
40 matches (two double round robins: each side hosts each other side twice, 16 matches per team),
about 80,000 events. The season goes to `data/generated/` (git-ignored, regenerate any time). One
scripted demo match goes to `data/showcase/` (committed). Realism v2 (fixtures, squads,
substitutions, the ball model, dead balls and time) is described below; the measured realism is in
`docs/realism-report.md` (`python -m tools.realism_report`).

## Pitch frame

`x` runs 0-100 toward the opponent goal, `y` runs 0-100 across the pitch. **Every event is stored in the
acting team's attacking frame**, so `left_flank` (y < 20) always means the left of the team attacking.
A defender's weak side is also stored from the attacker's point of view: `weak_lane = left_flank`
means attackers coming down *their* left find that defence soft (the defender's own right).

Lanes: left_flank 0-20, left_half_space 20-40, center 40-60, right_half_space 60-80, right_flank 80-100.
Final third: x >= 66.7. Box: x >= 84 and 22 <= y <= 78.

Clock: `clock_sec` is elapsed time from kick-off and runs on through stoppage time and the half-time
whistle, so the second half starts at roughly 2,800-3,000 s and a match ends at roughly 5,700-6,300 s.
`period` is 1 or 2 and `minute`/`second` follow football convention: the minute restarts at 45 in the
second half, so stoppage time shows as 45+ or 90+ (`minute` 47 in period 1 is 45+2). A `half_end`
event closes each half. Use `period` and `minute` for match time; use `clock_sec` for ordering and
durations.

## Tables

**teams.csv** team_id, code, name, style_seed (hidden ground truth), lane_weights, weak_lane, att, dfn,
press, tempo, directness, sit_deep, attacking_output, counter, shot_spam, weak_strength (scales the
weak-lane bonus, blank without a weak lane), formation (4-3-3, 4-2-3-1, 4-4-2, 3-5-2, 4-1-4-1)

`sit_deep` (0-1) is the defensive block only: the share of possessions a leading team spends protecting
the lead, and how hard its block is to enter. `attacking_output` (around 1.0) is chance-creation volume
(shot appetite and box-entry success), set independently, so a team's goal output is not derived from
how deep it sits.

**players.csv** player_id, team_id, name, shirt, position (GK, DF, MF, FW), role, home_x, home_y,
first_choice (1 = in the formation XI), finishing, usage, set_piece_taker, trap_flag. Squads of 24: the
formation XI plus 2 goalkeepers, 4 defenders, 4 midfielders and 3 forwards in reserve (9 of them on
each match-day bench).
Names come from one shuffled pool per league, so no two players share a full name.

**matches.csv** match_id, season, matchweek, date, kickoff, home_team_id, away_team_id, home_score,
away_score, home_xg, away_xg, ball_in_play_sec. Fixtures: circle method, home and away alternating so
no side has more than 2 home or 2 away matches in a row; weekend slots from 15 Aug 2026 with
international breaks and two midweek rounds.

**lineups.csv** match_id, team_id, player_id, role, starter, on_sec, off_sec (elapsed clock; blank for
an unused substitute), minutes_played, sent_off. 11 starters and 9 substitutes per side; 2-5
substitutions, mostly between 55' and 85', earlier when trailing or after a red card. A red card (or
a fifth yellow) bans the player from the team's next match. Light rotation after a short rest;
injuries force a substitution. Trap players and goalkeepers are never rested or taken off for
tactical reasons.

**events.csv** event_id, match_id, period, minute, second, clock_sec, team_id, player_id, opponent_team_id,
event_type, x_start, y_start, x_end, y_end, outcome, body_part, play_pattern, under_pressure, possession_id,
sequence_id, sequence_index, score_home, score_away, related_player_id

- `event_type` and `outcome`:
  - on the ball: pass (success, fail, offside), carry, dribble (success, fail), shot (goal, saved,
    blocked, off_target)
  - winning it: tackle (success, fail), interception, ball_recovery, possession_change, duel
    (success, fail; aerial or ground, `body_part` head for aerial), clearance, block
  - goalkeeper: save (held, parried), claim
  - dead balls and discipline: foul (free_kick, penalty), card (yellow, second_yellow, red), offside,
    injury (off), substitution (tactical, fatigue, injury), half_end
- `related_player_id`: the player fouled (foul), the player coming on (substitution), otherwise blank.
- `play_pattern`: open_play, counter, set_piece (corners and free kicks), kickoff, throw_in, goal_kick;
  it describes the whole sequence that the restart starts.
- `pressure` events are logged by the defending team in their own frame; `under_pressure` marks the passer.
- A failed pass ends in an interception, a block, a tackle on the receiver, going out of play (a
  throw-in, corner or goal kick follows) or a loose ball (a ball_recovery by either side).
- Continuity: a player's consecutive events in a half are never further apart than 6 m + 8 m/s of
  elapsed time, and every open-play on-ball action starts where the ball last was
  (`tests/test_realism.py`).

**passes.csv** (one row per pass event) event_id, match_id, passer_id, recipient_player_id, pass_distance_m,
pass_angle_rad, pass_type (short, long_ball, through_ball, cross, corner, free_kick, throw_in, goal_kick),
pass_difficulty (1 - modelled success probability), ball_speed_kmh, is_forward (x gain >= 8),
is_progressive (x gain >= 10), is_key_pass, xa

**shots.csv** (one row per shot event) event_id, match_id, team_id, player_id, shot_distance_m, shot_angle_rad,
shot_type (open_play, header, set_piece, free_kick, penalty), assist_event_id, is_big_chance, shot_result
(goal, saved, blocked, off_target), xg, shot_speed_kmh. xG: a logistic model of distance and angle,
lower for headers and set pieces, 0.78 for a penalty; calibrated by bin (`docs/realism-report.md`).

**ground_truth.json** each team's planted style, planted player traps, and for the showcase match the
measured trap evidence with event IDs. Only tests read it; `engine/` and `agents/` never do.
Also `expected` (season calibration targets with ranges and sources, from `data_gen/calibration.py`)
and `measured` (the same metrics on this season), plus `showcase.player_overrides`.

## The teams (fictional)

| Code | Hidden style seed |
|---|---|
| AUR | left-heavy counter, protects a lead by sitting deep |
| NOR | slow possession, soft against attacks down the attacker's left, shoots from range when chasing |
| CAL | high press, central play |
| VES | wing play down both flanks |
| HAR | direct long-ball, shoots early and often |

## Planted traps

| Trap | Where it lives | What the data shows |
|---|---|---|
| Field tilt while leading | AUR leads, NOR chases (any match; strongest in the showcase) | the chaser holds ~70% of final-third passes but makes very few box entries |
| Shot spam | NOR when chasing a deep block | a dozen-plus shots at ~0.03 xG each |
| Volume passer | NOR_07 (right winger) | season: a top-3 forward passer (not a runaway leader), ~90% ending on the flanks; showcase: the strong version, clear leader in the match |
| Corner specialist | NOR_11 (left winger) | most of his chances created come from corners |
| One-flank attack vs weak side | AUR attack vs NOR defence | AUR sends ~60% of entries down its left; NOR is soft on exactly that side |

## Calibration

Targets live in `data_gen/calibration.py` (also in `ground_truth.json` under `expected`, with the
measured values under `measured`). Era: recent Premier League seasons (2022/23-2024/25). Every target
is a range with a source note: a public page (URL in `SOURCES`) or "approximate" (a wide
football-knowledge range). A measured value passes inside [lo - a, hi + a], a = 2 sd / sqrt(n), so a
40-match season gets a fair allowance; season-shape targets (table spread, cross-team correlations,
players used) need the 20-team league. `NOT_YET_MODELLED` (targets the generator does not meet yet)
is empty after realism v2. Tuning changes behaviour parameters only, never scores or events.

## Showcase overrides

The showcase match uses the season simulator and players, with `SHOWCASE_PLAYER_TRAITS` applied on
top (NOR's right winger's usage and NOR's left winger's corner delivery), so the demo shows the
unmistakable version of a trap while the
season keeps the realistic one. The overrides are listed in `ground_truth.json`.

## What is deliberately not in the data

Tracking data (player positions, speeds, distance covered) and anything derived from it. Positions
exist only inside the simulator (to keep events continuous); the data carries event locations only.
Ball and shot speed are generated as event attributes. No VAR, no penalty shoot-outs, no weather.

## How the sim plants traps

Traps are emergent from behaviour rules, not patched in afterwards: a leading team drops deep, cedes the
final third and counters; the chasing team circulates around the block; a `wide_forward` player sends
forward passes to his flank; a set-piece taker delivers every corner. The showcase match uses the same
simulator (plus the showcase overrides above) with one scripted early goal, then searches seeds until all
five traps appear in the one match.

The field-tilt detector fires only when the chaser's box entries are low (<= 15% of its final-third
passes). A genuinely dominant side such as CAL also holds most of the final third but keeps entering
the box, so its spells while level or ahead do not fire (tested as a false-positive control).

## Showcase margins

Every showcase trap threshold must be cleared by at least 10% (`SHOWCASE_MIN_MARGIN`). The margins are
recorded in `ground_truth.json` under `showcase.trap_margins`. The seed search starts from a recorded
offset (`SHOWCASE_SEARCH_START`) to save about two minutes per generation; a slow test re-runs the search
from zero and checks it finds the same seed.

## Full league (`--league full`)

`python -m data_gen.generate --league full` writes a 20-team league to `data/full/` (git-ignored):
double round robin by the circle method (380 matches, 38 per team, one match per team per matchweek),
about 800,000 events (800,670 for seed 2026), about 120 MB, about 15 s on a laptop. `--league small` (the default) is byte-identical to the
5-team output and still writes the showcase; `full` does not touch the showcase.

- The five original teams are unchanged. The 15 extra teams (`data_gen/league.py`) are drawn from six
  archetypes (high press, low-block counter, possession build-up, direct long ball, wing play, balanced)
  with seeded variation (`FULL_LEAGUE_TEAM_SEED`) in lane weights, att/dfn, press, tempo, directness,
  sit_deep, attacking_output, counter and shot_spam. Names and codes are invented.
- `teams.csv` gains two columns in the full league: `weak_strength` (scales the weak-lane bonus;
  1.0 = NOR's original plant) and `archetype`.
- Extra plants: volume passers GRM_07, QNT_11, KWC_07; corner specialists ESF_11, MDL_07, ASV_11; shot-spam
  teams ESF, LMF; sit-deep-when-leading teams DCH, JPH; soft lanes OKP (right_flank, 2.5), HBC (left_flank,
  2.0), MDL (right_half_space, 1.5), FWM (center, 1.0), RVD (left_half_space, 0.6), IRK (right_flank, 0.3).
- `ground_truth.json` (full) lists each team's archetype and parameters, `team_traps`, `planted_players`,
  and `expected_evidence`: what each plant should leave in the data. For example, shot spam shows only
  while trailing (opponent sitting deep), and a volume passer is his team's top forward passer and in the
  league's top 10% rather than the league leader.
