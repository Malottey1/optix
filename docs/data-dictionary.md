# Synthetic data dictionary

Generated by `python -m data_gen.generate --seed 2026` (deterministic). Five fictional teams, 40 matches
(each pair meets four times, 16 matches per team), about 74,000 events. The season goes to
`data/generated/` (git-ignored, regenerate any time). One scripted demo match goes to
`data/showcase/` (committed).

## Pitch frame

`x` runs 0-100 toward the opponent goal, `y` runs 0-100 across the pitch. **Every event is stored in the
acting team's attacking frame**, so `left_flank` (y < 20) always means the left of the team attacking.
A defender's weak side is also stored from the attacker's point of view: `weak_lane = left_flank`
means attackers coming down *their* left find that defence soft (the defender's own right).

Lanes: left_flank 0-20, left_half_space 20-40, center 40-60, right_half_space 60-80, right_flank 80-100.
Final third: x >= 66.7. Box: x >= 84 and 22 <= y <= 78. Match clock in seconds, second half from 2700.

## Tables

**teams.csv** team_id, code, name, style_seed (hidden ground truth), lane_weights, weak_lane, att, dfn, press, tempo, directness, sit_deep, attacking_output, counter, shot_spam

`sit_deep` (0-1) is the defensive block only: the share of possessions a leading team spends protecting
the lead, and how hard its block is to enter. `attacking_output` (around 1.0) is chance-creation volume
(shot appetite and box-entry success), set independently, so a team's goal output is not derived from
how deep it sits.

**players.csv** player_id, team_id, name, shirt, position, role, home_x, home_y, finishing, usage, set_piece_taker, trap_flag

**matches.csv** match_id, season, matchweek, date, home_team_id, away_team_id, home_score, away_score, home_xg, away_xg

**events.csv** event_id, match_id, period, minute, second, clock_sec, team_id, player_id, opponent_team_id,
event_type, x_start, y_start, x_end, y_end, outcome, body_part, play_pattern, under_pressure, possession_id,
sequence_id, sequence_index, score_home, score_away

- `event_type`: pass, shot, tackle, interception, ball_recovery, pressure, possession_change
- `play_pattern`: open_play, counter, set_piece, kickoff
- `pressure` events are logged by the defending team in their own frame; `under_pressure` marks the passer

**passes.csv** (one row per pass event) event_id, match_id, passer_id, recipient_player_id, pass_distance_m,
pass_angle_rad, pass_type (short, long_ball, through_ball, cross, corner), pass_difficulty (1 - modelled
success probability), ball_speed_kmh, is_forward (x gain >= 8), is_progressive (x gain >= 10), is_key_pass, xa

**shots.csv** (one row per shot event) event_id, match_id, team_id, player_id, shot_distance_m, shot_angle_rad,
shot_type (open_play, header, set_piece), assist_event_id, is_big_chance, shot_result (goal, saved, blocked,
off_target), xg, shot_speed_kmh

**ground_truth.json** each team's planted style, planted player traps, and for the showcase match the
measured trap evidence with event IDs. Tests and, later, the engine's own checks compare against it.
Also `expected` (season calibration targets with tolerances, from `data_gen/calibration.py`) and
`measured` (the same metrics on this season), plus `showcase.player_overrides`.

## The teams (fictional)

| Code | Hidden style seed |
|---|---|
| AUR | left-heavy counter, protects a lead by sitting deep |
| NOR | slow possession, soft against attacks down the attacker's left, shoots from range when chasing |
| CAL | high press, central play |
| VES | wing play down both flanks |
| HAR | direct long-ball, shoots early and often |

## Planted traps

| Trap | Where it lives | What the data shows |
|---|---|---|
| Field tilt while leading | AUR leads, NOR chases (any match; strongest in the showcase) | the chaser holds ~70% of final-third passes but makes very few box entries |
| Shot spam | NOR when chasing a deep block | a dozen-plus shots at ~0.03 xG each |
| Volume passer | NOR_07 (right winger) | season: a top-3 forward passer (not a runaway leader), ~90% ending on the flanks; showcase: the strong version, clear leader in the match |
| Corner specialist | NOR_11 (left winger) | most of his chances created come from corners |
| One-flank attack vs weak side | AUR attack vs NOR defence | AUR sends ~60% of entries down its left; NOR is soft on exactly that side |

## Calibration

The simulator is tuned to season-level targets written down before tuning (`data_gen/calibration.py`,
also in `ground_truth.json` under `expected`): home/draw/away 38/30/32% (+-15 points), 2.7 goals per
match (2.2-3.2), 70% of matches decided by one goal or fewer (+-15), 22-26 shots and 8-13 corners per
match. Tolerances are wide because a season is 40 matches. Tuning changes behaviour parameters only
(shot rates, home edge, game-state effects, team `attacking_output`), never scores or events. Streak rules
and scoreline caps are deliberately not targets: they come from tiny samples.

Game-state behaviour: a leader protects its lead on a share of possessions equal to its `sit_deep`;
a trailing side's shot appetite and box-entry success ramp up as time runs out; the home side gets a
small passing edge and a few more chances.

## Showcase overrides

The showcase match uses the season simulator and players, with `SHOWCASE_PLAYER_TRAITS` applied on
top (currently NOR_07's usage), so the demo shows the unmistakable version of a trap while the
season keeps the realistic one. The overrides are listed in `ground_truth.json`.

## What is deliberately not in the data

Tracking data (player positions, speeds, distance covered), substitutions, cards and fouls. Those are
later additions. Ball and shot speed are generated as event attributes.

## How the sim plants traps

Traps are emergent from behaviour rules, not patched in afterwards: a leading team drops deep, cedes the
final third and counters; the chasing team circulates around the block; a `wide_forward` player sends
forward passes to his flank; a set-piece taker delivers every corner. The showcase match uses the same
simulator (plus the showcase overrides above) with one scripted early goal, then searches seeds until all
five traps appear in the one match.

The field-tilt detector fires only when the chaser's box entries are low (<= 15% of its final-third
passes). A genuinely dominant side such as CAL also holds most of the final third but keeps entering
the box, so its spells while level or ahead do not fire (tested as a false-positive control).

## Showcase margins

Every showcase trap threshold must be cleared by at least 10% (`SHOWCASE_MIN_MARGIN`). The margins are
recorded in `ground_truth.json` under `showcase.trap_margins`. The seed search starts from a recorded
offset (`SHOWCASE_SEARCH_START`) to save about two minutes per generation; a slow test re-runs the search
from zero and checks it finds the same seed.

## Full league (`--league full`)

`python -m data_gen.generate --league full` writes a 20-team league to `data/full/` (git-ignored):
double round robin by the circle method (380 matches, 38 per team, one match per team per matchweek),
about 680,000 events, about 100 MB, about 17 s. `--league small` (the default) is byte-identical to the
5-team output and still writes the showcase; `full` does not touch the showcase.

- The five original teams are unchanged. The 15 extra teams (`data_gen/league.py`) are drawn from six
  archetypes (high press, low-block counter, possession build-up, direct long ball, wing play, balanced)
  with seeded variation (`FULL_LEAGUE_TEAM_SEED`) in lane weights, att/dfn, press, tempo, directness,
  sit_deep, attacking_output, counter and shot_spam. Names and codes are invented.
- `teams.csv` gains two columns in the full league: `weak_strength` (scales the weak-lane bonus;
  1.0 = NOR's original plant) and `archetype`.
- Extra plants: volume passers GRM_07, QNT_11, KWC_07; corner specialists ESF_11, MDL_07, ASV_11; shot-spam
  teams ESF, LMF; sit-deep-when-leading teams DCH, JPH; soft lanes OKP (right_flank, 2.5), HBC (left_flank,
  2.0), MDL (right_half_space, 1.5), FWM (center, 1.0), RVD (left_half_space, 0.6), IRK (right_flank, 0.3).
- `ground_truth.json` (full) lists each team's archetype and parameters, `team_traps`, `planted_players`,
  and `expected_evidence`: what each plant should leave in the data. For example, shot spam shows only
  while trailing (opponent sitting deep), and a volume passer is his team's top forward passer and in the
  league's top 10% rather than the league leader.
